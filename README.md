# Hi, I'm Riccardo Sindoni

Finance and administration professional in retail, moving into Data Analytics. Based in Turin, Italy.

I work in finance and administration for a group of retail companies: cash planning, supplier payments, leases, reporting and coordination between entities. Day to day I see where decisions are slowed down by scattered data, so I build small, practical tools that turn operational data into something a manager can act on: dashboards, forecasting models and simple automations.

I build these projects with AI assistants (Claude, by Anthropic) and I say so in every repository. I define the problem, the business rules and the checks; the AI helps me write the code, and I review and test the result.
